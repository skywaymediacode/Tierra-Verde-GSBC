One Dock. A Different Kind of Day.

Where the West Coast Quiets Down.

From 240 Madonna Blvd, the Gulf opens up fast. Shell Key Preserve, Bunces Pass, Egmont Key, Fort De Soto — all of it within a short cruise of the same slip. Tampa has the skyline. Tierra Verde has the silence.

Shell Key & Bunces Pass

Anchor Down. Slow Down.

Shell Key Preserve sits just minutes from the dock — crystal-clear water, soft sand, and the kind of sandbar afternoon you'll remember. Bunces Pass is the local favorite for quieter water and an easy anchor. Dolphins are regulars out here.

Egmont Key & the Gulf

Snorkel the Ruins of Fort Dade

A few miles offshore, Egmont Key delivers reef-clear snorkeling over the submerged gun batteries of an abandoned 1900s fort. Borrow snorkel gear from the club on your way out — we keep it on hand for members.

Dockside Dining

Tie Up at Island Grille

Island Grille & Raw Bar is right at the marina — dockside seating, sunset views, and a menu that earns its name. For brunch, point the boat toward Buoy's Waterfront Grill in St. Pete. Order the short rib Benedict. Thank us later.

A Marina That Feels Like Home Base

You're Not Just Renting. You're Welcomed.

Cindy, Logan, and Davis run a tight ship — and members tell us so in every review. New to the water? You'll go through a quick orientation so you're confident from your first reservation. Already an experienced boater? They'll just point you toward the best windows for the day and get out of your way.

Local knowledge matters here. The Gulf is generous when you know it, less so when you don't. Our team checks tides, weather, and Bay conditions every morning. You'll get an honest read on the day — where it's calm, where the dolphins are running, and when to head back ahead of the afternoon chop.

Want a captain? Available on request. Want to just go? Even easier.
